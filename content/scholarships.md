International applicants with an external scholarship receive preference in STAI lab selection. A scholarship is not required to apply or to be accepted.

In the application form linked from your [position’s opening page](/opening/), tell us which scholarships you already hold and which you plan to apply for. For each award, give its name, status, funding amount, and duration, if known. For planned applications, include the expected application and result dates. If you have no scholarship or application plan, write “None”.

## Scholarships by citizenship

These are options to check for graduate study at KAIST, not a guarantee of eligibility or an open application round. Follow the provider’s current call for your degree, field, nationality, and start date. For an integrated MSc-PhD, confirm that the provider covers the integrated route and how long it will fund you.

## Other nationalities and GKS

Check your home government’s overseas-study scholarships and other sponsors that fund study at KAIST. The list above is not exhaustive.

[Global Korea Scholarship (GKS)](https://admission.kaist.ac.kr/intl-graduate/Admission/GKS) provides tuition and living support. Before choosing it, check whether the current university and department list includes the **Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI** for your degree and track. We have not confirmed that inclusion for the 2027 intake. KAIST’s participation alone does not establish eligibility for our graduate school. Check the nationality rules and Korean-language training requirements too.

## KAIST scholarships

The general [KAIST Scholarship](https://admission.kaist.ac.kr/intl-graduate/FinancialSupport/Scholarship/KAISTScholarship) is separate from an external award. Its living stipend may depend on research participation and advisor funding. KGPS and KPS are competitive KAIST awards; automatic consideration does not mean you have secured one. If you receive an award, include its confirmed terms in your application.

The degree scholarships above do not establish funding for an internship or postdoc. Applicants for those positions can report any relevant fellowship or sponsor in the same form.

Guidance checked on 16 October 2026. Use the linked official calls for current terms and deadlines.
