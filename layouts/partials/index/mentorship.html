The support behind your research

Direct mentorship. Room to become independent.

Strong papers grow from good questions, careful experiments and constructive feedback. We help you develop all three, while building the confidence to lead your own research.

1:1 time with Joon

Graduate students meet directly with Joon to discuss their research. Bring your ideas, results and open questions, and work through the next steps together.

Extra guidance when you need it

Students can book additional meetings with Joon. Get feedback when a project needs a fresh perspective, an experiment raises questions or a paper needs a clearer story.

Ownership from idea to paper

Learn to frame a question, design experiments and communicate what you discover. Our graduate programmes centre on first-author projects; interns work closely with a PhD student host.

Joon's Principles describe our approach to research and researcher development, including a PhD roadmap with clear milestones and growing independence.
