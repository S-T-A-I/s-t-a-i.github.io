STAI Lab - KAIST AI

AI that people can guide, correct and trust.

We study AI safety, agents, memory and retrieval at KAIST AI, led by Seong Joon Oh. Our approach separates the knowledge a model holds from its reasoning, so people can trace, update and control that knowledge.

We are recruiting interns, MSc and PhD students and postdocs. How to join ↓

Research

  • AI safety and human oversight

    Make AI useful without losing privacy or human control.

  • Agents and system evaluation

    Improve the whole system, from planning and tool use to coordination between agents.

  • Memory and continual adaptation

    Help agents learn from new information and user corrections without retraining for every update.

  • Retrieval and grounded reasoning

    Connect models to relevant, current evidence across text and images.

Compositional Generalization Requires Linear, Orthogonal Representations in Vision Embedding Models

Vision models can only recognise familiar parts in new combinations if their representations split into near-orthogonal pieces, one per concept. CLIP, SigLIP and DINO show this structure in part, and the models that show more of it generalise better.
