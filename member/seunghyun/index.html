Biography

I majored in Computer Science and Business Administration at Hanyang University. I am currently working as an intern researcher at KAIST STAI LAB under Professor Seong Joon Oh, collaborating on research with Ph.D. student Arnas Uselis.

I aim to deeply understand representation spaces, as I believe a solid grasp of these spaces and how models learn is fundamental to achieving AI safety. I enjoy studying the learning and reasoning mechanisms of different models, and I am especially interested in how models can fully integrate multimodal data.
