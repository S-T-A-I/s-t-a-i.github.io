Biography

I’m first-year Ph.D. student at KAIST STAI Lab, advised by Seongjoon Oh. I previously completed my Master’s at KAIST AI, where I was advised by Minjoon Seo. I received my B.S. in CS from KAIST.

My long-term goal is to build models that reason beyond the human data distribution. To this end, I study the learning signals, data structures, and evaluation methods that enable models to self-train and generalize systematically in multi-step reasoning. Currently, I am interested in iterative RL post-training and agentic systems, particularly autonomous research agents.
