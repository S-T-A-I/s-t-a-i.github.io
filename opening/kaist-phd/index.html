PhD

What you will do

We expect PhD students to run their own first-author projects, with possible collaborations with both senior and junior members inside and outside the lab.

Scholarships for international applicants

International applicants with an external scholarship receive preference. A scholarship is not required to apply or to be accepted. In the application form, describe any scholarship you hold and your plans to apply for funding. You may write “None”. See our scholarship guidance by citizenship.

How to apply

  1. 1

    Apply

    Fill in the STAI application form and attach your CV and transcript.

  2. 2

    Coffee chat

    An informal call with Seong Joon to see whether our interests fit.

  3. 3

    Interview

    Half a day. Present your past work to the lab (30 minutes plus questions), meet two lab members one-on-one, then discuss research directions with Seong Joon. We arrange the times by email.

  4. 4

    Offer

    We tell you the outcome by the offer date for your intake.

After the offer, apply through KAIST Graduate Admissions and name Seong Joon Oh as your intended supervisor.

We accept applications only through the STAI application form. We do not accept applications by email or social media.

Apply at least a month before the offer date for your intake. This leaves time for the chat and the interview.

Offer dates by intake
  • International spring: 31 August
  • International autumn, early track: 30 November
  • International autumn, regular track: 28 February
  • Domestic spring: 30 June
  • Domestic autumn: 31 March
