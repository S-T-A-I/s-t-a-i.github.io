Postdoc

What you will do

We expect postdocs to be independent researchers who can manage multiple research threads in the lab through supervision, while pursuing their own first-author agenda. A research vision broader than a single project is expected.

Scholarships for international applicants

International applicants with an external scholarship receive preference. A scholarship is not required to apply or to be accepted. In the application form, describe any scholarship you hold and your plans to apply for funding. You may write “None”. See our scholarship guidance by citizenship.

How to apply

  1. 1

    Apply

    Fill in the STAI application form and attach your CV.

  2. 2

    Coffee chat

    An informal call with Seong Joon to see whether our interests fit.

  3. 3

    Interview

    An interview day at the lab. We support travel, and the interview can be remote if travel is not feasible. Present your past work to the lab (30 minutes plus questions), meet four or more lab members one-on-one, discuss future research with Seong Joon, then join the lab for a meal.

  4. 4

    Offer

    We tell you the outcome after the interview.

We accept applications only through the STAI application form. We do not accept applications by email or social media.
