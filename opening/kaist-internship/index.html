Internship

Start your research career

Undergraduate students are welcome to apply. Join a research project, work closely with a PhD student and build experience towards contributing to a research paper. Explore our publication record to see the questions we work on and the conferences where we publish.

What you will do

We expect interns to participate in a predefined research agenda as a co-author, working closely with their PhD student host.

Supervision

Your day-to-day supervisor is the PhD student you apply to work with. They define the research direction, set milestones, and provide regular feedback. Joon is available for broader guidance but does not manage the internship on a daily basis. Choose your PhD student host carefully - your internship experience depends largely on this match.

Scholarships for international applicants

International applicants with an external scholarship receive preference. A scholarship is not required to apply or to be accepted. In the application form, describe any scholarship you hold and your plans to apply for funding. You may write “None”. See our scholarship guidance by citizenship.

KAIST AI Research Internship (KAIRI)

The Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI also runs KAIRI, a research internship each summer and winter. You can apply to KAIRI and name STAI as your preferred lab, in addition to the STAI application form. KAIRI is open to domestic applicants. International students at overseas universities apply through the school’s visiting internship. Calls and deadlines are announced on the school website.

How to apply

  1. 1

    Apply

    Fill in the STAI application form. Choose the PhD student you would like to work with and attach your CV.

  2. 2

    Coffee chat

    An informal call with that PhD student.

  3. 3

    Interview

    One hour with the PhD student. Present your past work for about 10 minutes and discuss it. Then talk about research ideas that combine your strengths with our direction.

  4. 4

    Offer

    We tell you the outcome after the interview.

We accept applications only through the STAI application form. We do not accept applications by email or social media.
