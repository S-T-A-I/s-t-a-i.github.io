Openings

Scholarships for international applicants

International applicants with an external scholarship receive preference in STAI lab selection. A scholarship is not required to apply or to be accepted.

In the application form linked from your position’s opening page, tell us which scholarships you already hold and which you plan to apply for. For each award, give its name, status, funding amount, and duration, if known. For planned applications, include the expected application and result dates. If you have no scholarship or application plan, write “None”.

Scholarships by citizenship

These are options to check for graduate study at KAIST, not a guarantee of eligibility or an open application round. Follow the provider’s current call for your degree, field, nationality, and start date. For an integrated MSc-PhD, confirm that the provider covers the integrated route and how long it will fund you.

Indonesia

LPDP. Master’s and doctoral funding, including tuition and living expenses. Check the current STEM call, approved programme, full or partial funding option, and return-to-Indonesia obligations.

Kazakhstan

Bolashak. Tuition and living support for overseas postgraduate study. KAIST is listed by its admissions office as a recognised university. Check the current approved fields, selection requirements, and return-service obligations.

Vietnam

Vingroup doctoral scholarship. Doctoral funding for science and technology, including tuition and living expenses. KAIST is on the target-university list. From the 2026 cohort, new applications are for doctoral degrees only.

Spain and Portugal

la Caixa postgraduate fellowships abroad. Postgraduate funding that includes South Korea. Check the nationality, prior-degree location and completion-date requirements, eligible start dates, and maximum award duration.

Taiwan

Taiwan-KAIST scholarship. For new doctoral applicants. KAIST lists tuition and an annual stipend. Check the Ministry of Education call for citizenship and residence requirements, award duration, and application dates.

KAIST lists these programmes in its external scholarship guidance. Vingroup also publishes its target universities and doctoral eligibility.

Other nationalities and GKS

Check your home government’s overseas-study scholarships and other sponsors that fund study at KAIST. The list above is not exhaustive.

Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) provides tuition and living support. Before choosing it, check whether the current university and department list includes the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI for your degree and track. We have not confirmed that inclusion for the 2027 intake. KAIST’s participation alone does not establish eligibility for our graduate school. Check the nationality rules and Korean-language training requirements too.

KAIST scholarships

The general KAIST Scholarship is separate from an external award. Its living stipend may depend on research participation and advisor funding. KGPS and KPS are competitive KAIST awards; automatic consideration does not mean you have secured one. If you receive an award, include its confirmed terms in your application.

The degree scholarships above do not establish funding for an internship or postdoc. Applicants for those positions can report any relevant fellowship or sponsor in the same form.

Guidance checked on 16 October 2026. Use the linked official calls for current terms and deadlines.
